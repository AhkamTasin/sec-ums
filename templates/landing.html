About the Institution

Engineering education
with purpose, in Sylhet.

Sylhet Engineering College (SEC) educates the next generation of engineers through hands-on laboratories, industry-oriented coursework and close faculty mentorship. The college runs undergraduate programmes across multiple engineering disciplines, with a strong focus on practical skills and research culture.

The SEC University Management System is the college's official digital backbone — unifying admissions records, course management, attendance, examination results, fee collection and the central library into a single secure portal for students, faculty and administrative staff.
